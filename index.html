The problem

Two hundred people on the same dance floor. You went home without meeting one.

Como funciona

Do feed até a pista, em três passos.

Tudo gira em torno de uma regra: você só combina com quem confirmou presença na mesma festa que você.

How it works

From the feed to the dance floor, in three steps.

Everything turns on one rule: you only match with people who confirmed they're going to the same party as you.

Escolha a festa

Pick the party

Faça check-in

Check in

Confirm you're going and join the guest list. Checking in is what unlocks everything else — without it, nobody shows up for you.

Match with who's there
