Feedback & Suggestions

Your input shapes NightHive.

We're building NightHive around what our community actually needs. Found a bug? Have an idea for a new feature? Something felt confusing? We want to hear about it.

To help us respond faster, try to include:

  • What happened, and what you expected to happen instead;
  • Which screen in the app (if it's a bug);
  • A screenshot, if you have one.
Send feedback by email

Prefer to leave a review instead?

A rating on the App Store or Google Play helps us a lot too — and it's the fastest way for other people to discover NightHive.
